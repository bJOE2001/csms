## Final generation prompts

### Hero

Use case: product-mockup. Generate a single photorealistic commercial coffee photograph for the hero of an independent street-inspired premium café website. Portrait 4:5 composition. A single unbranded clear disposable takeaway cup of iced latte on a softly textured warm cream concrete café ledge, dark espresso brown café background, warm side-window daylight, soft believable shadows, shallow depth of field. The whole cup and simple flat takeaway lid fully visible with comfortable breathing room around it. Cup centered and photographed straight-on at approximately cup middle height so the central front-facing surface is nearly flat and unobstructed; no hand, no straw crossing it. Honest coffee and milk marbling, a few realistic droplets, natural ice, matte authentic surroundings, premium editorial 50mm product photography, restrained youthful urban café atmosphere. Cup occupies about 70 percent of image height and 50 percent of image width. Keep the central front area of the cup clean and completely blank for a separately composited official sticker. Absolutely no logo, no lettering, no text, no watermark, no invented packaging markings, no glass drinking vessel, no steam, no floating objects, no scattered coffee beans. This is a temporary packaging concept, not actual shop photography.

### Lifestyle

Use case: photorealistic-natural. A single commercial editorial lifestyle photograph, landscape 5:4 composition, for an independent premium street-inspired coffee website. A quiet, authentic neighborhood café moment: close view of two people's hands resting naturally at a small warm timber table near a sunlit window, with two unbranded disposable takeaway coffee cups, one matte cream paper hot-coffee cup with a plain dark takeaway lid prominently in the lower middle foreground and one iced coffee in a clear disposable plastic takeaway cup farther back. No ceramic or glass drinking cups. Cropped people, no faces required. Muted cream, espresso brown and caramel palette, warm late-afternoon daylight, believable soft shadows, natural skin and paper textures, shallow depth of field, 50mm documentary-style professional photography. Entire foreground cup visible and front almost straight-on, center face unobstructed and blank, no exaggerated condensation or steam. Restrained modern neighborhood café interior suggested softly in background, not a recognizable real business. Absolutely no logos, text, lettering, fake packaging marks, watermarks, scattered beans or decorative graphics. These are unbranded temporary visual concepts, not photographs of an actual shop, and an official wordmark may later be composited as a separate exact asset.
